Why it exists

A chat piles up in one line. Work branches.

You talk, wander off on a side question, come back, decide, and the same file changes three times. Scrolling alone will not tell you where you decided something or what you left out.

As a chat

  • I want a bot that alerts me on exchange rates
  • How is polling different from a webhook?
  • Polling it is, then
  • Compare with my target and send an alert
  • Just change the threshold to 1,380

One column, top to bottom. A decision looks the same as a detour.

As a Gadak map

The main line and the side trip split apart. You can see what was decided and where you are.

What Gadak does

It reads, sorts, and speaks up about what you missed.

Gadak stays beside the conversation. It never answers for the model. It draws the flow.

One question is one station. A decision is a large dot with a bold name, a side trip is a grey branch that drops below the line, and a red dot shows where you are. Click a station to jump to that moment.

A file that changed without being asked, a request that never got an answer, the context to carry into a new chat. Gadak rereads the record, lists only what it could verify, and keeps a trace of how it decided.

Every project is a line with its own colour, laid over the last 7, 15 or 30 days. Conversations that continue each other are joined as transfers, and clicking a line opens its strip map.

On a Mac, a Gadak button rests at the edge of the screen. Click it and the map of your current conversation appears over whatever you are working in, with small buttons for to-dos, decisions and files. You choose which buttons it shows.

Press Look ahead and Gadak reads what you are trying to do, searches your past records and the documents in your working folder, and offers up to three ways forward. They are questions to take to your LLM, not answers. A path with nothing found behind it is not offered.

Private and in your hands

Your chats stay on your computer. Sending stays with you.

Conversations are stored on your computer

What Gadak reads lives only in the .gadak folder on this machine. Only while sorting does a slice of that turn (the start of the question, the ends of the answer, the names and line counts of changed files) go to the AI you connected. API keys are kept only in the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager.

Gadak does not answer

There is no chat box in Gadak. It prepares what to say and hands it to you; you send it. Only the two kinds you switch on (undoing an unrequested change, and a summary to carry on) are sent by Gadak itself.

Three stops

Leave it on and it reads by itself.

  1. 1

    Install

    On a Mac, drag Gadak from the disk image into Applications. On Windows, open the installer. No Python, no terminal.

  2. 2

    Connect an AI

    Pick one AI for the sorting: a Claude subscription, a ChatGPT subscription, or an LLM API key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini).

  3. 3

    Leave it on

    Gadak finds the Claude Code and Codex conversations on this computer and sorts the one you are looking at first. Web chats on Claude and ChatGPT are read through a Chrome extension.

Questions

Before you download

Is it free?

Yes. Gadak is a free app made as a class project, and the code is public on GitHub. For the sorting it uses the subscription or API key you already have, so what Gadak uses counts against that plan's limit or that key's bill.

Which conversations does it read?

Claude Code and Codex conversations are read automatically while Gadak is on. Web chats on Claude and ChatGPT are read once you add the Chrome extension, and past conversations can be brought in at once from an export file. Cursor connects with one click on a Mac. ChatGPT on the web, Cursor and export files have not been verified end to end in real use yet. Chats in the desktop and mobile apps cannot be read directly.

Where do my conversations go?

The full text is stored only on your computer. While sorting, the start of that turn's question, the ends of the answer, and the names and line counts of changed files go to the company behind the AI you connected. A turn being checked for missed work sends a little more. The app you download here sends no usage records to the Gadak team.

Which AI do I need?

One of these: a Claude subscription (Pro or Max), a ChatGPT subscription (Plus or Pro), or an LLM API key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini). Choose it under AI 연결 (Connect AI) in the Gadak window. Without one, Gadak only reads and shows your conversations.

I get a warning on first launch

Gadak is a class project and has no paid signature from Apple or Microsoft, so the first launch needs one confirmation. On a Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On Windows: More info → Run anyway. You can check the file against the SHA-256 values on the download page.

What is different on Windows?

The Windows version is a first release. The makers have no Windows PC, so it was checked only under Wine on a Mac and has not been run on a real PC yet. The floating button, the one-click setup that fetches Claude Code or Codex for you, the Cursor connection with auto-send, and the Claude usage gauge are not there yet.

How far can I trust the sorting?

An AI does the sorting, so it can be wrong. In the conversation we measured it found six or seven side trips out of ten, and the Decided mark still wavers between runs. That is why a to-do appears only when Gadak has checked the evidence itself, and why it keeps a record of what it looked at.

How do I remove it?

On a Mac, move Gadak from Applications to the Trash. On Windows, uninstall it under Settings → Apps. To delete the conversations it read as well, remove the .gadak folder in your home folder.
