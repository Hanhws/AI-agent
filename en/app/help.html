GadakHelp

First 3 minutes

Gadak draws your conversations with an AI as a transit map and tells you first about what slipped past. Gadak does not answer. You keep chatting where you always do.

  1. Turn Gadak on. It reads the Claude Code and Codex chats on this MacPC by itself.
  2. Connect an AI for sorting. Pick one under Connect AI at the bottom left.
  3. Choose a chat on the left. The strip map appears at the top, the chat in the middle and the lists on the right.
  4. Click a station on the map. It jumps straight to where that was said.
Chat text is stored only on this MacPC. It goes to the AI you connected only while it is being sorted.

Connect AI

The AI Gadak uses to sort your chats. Pick one of three under Connect AI at the bottom left. Without one, Gadak only reads and shows your chats.

Claude subscription
Uses your Pro or Max subscription. No extra charge. Press Connect and sign in in your browser.
ChatGPT subscription
Uses your Plus or Pro subscription. No extra charge. Press Connect and sign in in your browser.
LLM API key
Paste an Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini API key. Usage is billed to that key. The key is kept only in the Keychain on this MacWindows Credential Manager on this PC.
Free Gemini key
A free key from Google AI Studio works too. It costs nothing, but the limit is low and what you send may be used by Google to improve its products.
Usage limit
With a subscription, Gadak shares the same limit as your own Claude or ChatGPT use.

Reading chats

Where Gadak reads chats from is shown under Sources at the bottom left.

Claude Code · Codex
Nothing to do. Turn Gadak on and it reads them by itself.
Cursor
Press Connect once under Sources.
Claude · ChatGPT on the web
Add the Gadak extension to Chrome once. How to add it is under Sources.
Past chats in bulk
Add the file you exported from each service with Choose file under Sources.
Remove from the list
Hover over a chat and press ×. Nothing is deleted; restore it at the bottom of the list.

Reading the map

One chat is one line. The left is the start, the right is now. Fold and unfold the strip map card at the top with Esc.

Main line
A thick line in the project’s colour. The same colour on the system map.
Station
A small black dot. One question and its answer. Click it to go there.
Decided
A large black dot with a bold station name. Something was decided there.
Now
A red dot with a glow. Where the chat is now.
Side trip
A grey line dropping 45° below. A question that wandered off for a while.
To do
A red ring below a station. Something is still left to do. Hover to see it.
Done
A grey ring. A to-do that is finished.
File
A small dot with a file name. A file was made or changed there.
Section
The label above the line. A stage of the conversation.
Folded section
Past sections shrink to ticks. Click the label to unfold.
Hidden branch
A dotted curve above a station. A trace of a question you edited and asked again.
Between chats
A grey dotted line. Joins one chat to the next when you view the whole project.
Transfer
The black arrow at the end of the line. Copies a summary of this chat to paste at the start of a new one.
System map
The button in the black bar at the top. Draws every project and chat from the last 30 days on one sheet. Switch with View as poster and close with Esc.
Day · Night
The switch on the right of the black bar at the top (also on the system map). Swaps between a light and a dark screen. Your choice is kept on this MacPC.

To-dos and lists

There are four tabs on the right. Type a word in the search box above them to search past chats too.

To do
Missed work and suggestions Gadak found. Press a button to copy text to send to the AI. To put one off, press Later.
Decided
Everything decided so far, newest first. Click one to go to where it was decided.
Files
Files that came out of the chat. A file changed several times is grouped into versions (v1 · v2).
Ahead
Ways forward from here that Gadak has scouted.

What shows up as a to-do

Missed request
Something you asked for that the answer left out.
Unrequested change
Something the AI changed without being asked.
Unfinished side trip
A side trip you came back from without deciding anything.
Carry on
When you open a new chat, paste in what was decided in the previous one.
Repeated question
Something you asked in another chat too. You can see that answer again.
Topic change
When several topics pile up in one chat, Gadak suggests moving to a new chat.
Next step
A path found by looking ahead.

What Gadak sends by itself

Turn these on below the To do tab. Only two kinds, undoing an unrequested change and a summary to carry on, are sent by Gadak itself, and only when you turn them on. For everything else it only prepares the text.

Ahead

Gadak reads where this chat is trying to go and finds up to three ways forward. They are questions to take to your AI, not answers.

Look ahead
The button at the top of the Ahead tab. Looks once, from where you are. Takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes.
Look ahead automatically
Turn it on below the To do tab. Looks by itself when you decide something or the section changes.
Carry on
A likely next step in the direction you were going.
Another way
A different approach you could try.
Prepare first
Something to check first, because it may trip you up if you carry on as you are.
See this path
Shows why this path and what was looked up. Press Ask this way to copy the text to send.
Each look uses a little of your AI usage limit (about 0.7% of a Claude Pro five-hour limit). That is why automatic look-ahead starts off.

Floating button

The Gadak button that rests on the edge of the screen in the Mac app. Not available yet in Gadak for Windows. It stays visible while you use other apps. Hover over it and buttons fan out around it.

Strip map
Shows the strip map of the chat you are in at the top of the screen. Press again to close it.
To do · Decided · Files
Opens a separate list window on that tab.
Search · Window
Opens the Gadak window. Search goes straight to the chat search box.
Change buttons
Right-click the Gadak button → Edit Buttons. Remove or add buttons and change their order. Up to six.
Project buttons
Add a project in Edit Buttons and that button opens the project’s strip map. Switch projects without opening the Gadak window.
Move
Drag the Gadak button to move it to another edge.
Close
Close floating windows with Esc.
Hide · Show again
Right-click the Gadak button → Hide Button. To show it again, use the Gadak button switch at the top right of the Gadak window, ⇧⌘B, the menu View → Floating Button, or right-click the Gadak icon in the Dock → Show Floating Button.

Glossary

The words Gadak uses. Type one in the search box above to find it.

Gadak
Korean for a strand. From “가닥을 잡다”, to get hold of the thread of something.
Line · Main line
The thick line drawing the flow of one chat.
Station
One question and its answer.
Section
A stage of the conversation. The label above the line.
Side trip
A question that wandered off from what you were talking about.
Decided
A mark that something was decided there.
File
A file made or changed in the chat.
Missed
Things that are easy to let slip. Missed requests, unrequested changes and unfinished side trips.
Suggestion
Something Gadak recommends. Carry on, repeated question, topic change and next step.
Note

Ahead

System map
A map of every project and chat on one sheet.
Sources
The panel showing where Gadak reads chats from. At the bottom left.
Sorting engine
The AI that sorts your chats. Choose it under Connect AI.
Decision trace
A record of what Gadak looked at when it decided something was a to-do.

When you’re stuck

First look at the line at the bottom left. It says what Gadak is doing now, or why it stopped.

Station names are just the start of my questions

No AI is connected for sorting. Pick one under Connect AI at the bottom left.

It says in red that it stopped

You hit the usage limit or were signed out. Once the limit resets, press Resume sorting and it carries on from where it stopped.

I pressed Look ahead and nothing appeared

Look at the line below the button. “Looking ahead” means just wait. “Found no path worth offering” means there was no path with evidence behind it. After “Could not look ahead” the reason is given.

I don’t see any chats

Check what was found under Sources. If you have never used an AI on this MacPC, there is nothing to read.

Chats I don’t need are mixed in

Hover over the chat and press ×. Restore it under “removed” at the bottom of the list.

A web chat doesn’t come in

Click the Gadak extension icon in Chrome. If Gadak is off, turn it on, and reload tabs you opened before adding the extension.

I can’t see the floating button

It is hidden. Turn on the Gadak button switch at the top right of the Gadak window, or right-click the Gadak icon in the Dock → Show Floating Button. If it still isn’t there, check all four edges of the screen.

The screen is too bright or too dark

Change it with the Day · Night switch on the right of the black bar at the top. If you don’t choose, it follows the MacWindows appearance setting.

The Mac blocks it the first time I open it

Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway near the bottom. You only need to do this once.

Windows blocks the installer

In the “Windows protected your PC” window, press More info → Run anyway.
